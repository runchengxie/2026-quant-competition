## Limitations

- The six-ETF sample is not a complete global asset universe.
- No FX conversion or hedge is applied; equal weighting in native currencies is not investable portfolio accounting.
- No real-time, bid/ask, depth, or order-level data is used.
- This research strategy is separate from the Hong Kong competition strategy and does not establish competition eligibility.
