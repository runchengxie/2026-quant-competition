# US CNN exploratory comparison

This page documents the two offline runs summarized on the site. It is not an accepted research dataset, a prospective holdout, a live track record, or an execution signal. No raw source prices or single-security benchmark curves are published here. The historical availability of the input and rights to publish its data remain unverified.

The source dataset `us-fixed-daily/20261021-v2` contains 10 currently selected US stocks plus SPY, with 953 aligned XNYS sessions between 2023-01-03 and 2026-10-20. IBKR TRADES daily bars are split adjusted and exclude dividends. Historical index membership was not reconstructed, so selection and survivorship bias remain. This dataset was previously examined and is not a fresh holdout.

The numeric walk-forward rank model uses a 400-session span including 80 validation sessions, a five-session label, five-session purge and embargo, and 63-session test windows. Nine actual evaluation windows comprise eight 58-session windows and one 20-session window. Each window starts in cash and liquidates independently. Close-based signals use the next session's open as a hypothetical fill; no actual fills, spreads, taxes or market impact are established. Costs of 0, 20 and 40 bps per side include entry, turnover and exit. Equal-weight and momentum comparisons select from the same fixed pool.

The matched numeric and image experiment uses the same 200 training rows and the same cross-sectional label, two filters and two epochs. The existing inclusive 30% threshold produces four positive labels on most dates; the portfolio still selects three. Architectures and calibration differ, so the comparison does not isolate image representation. The second run scales target equity weights to 40%, keeps the remainder in zero-interest cash and allows weights to drift on non-rebalance days. It matches nominal targets, not volatility or concentration.

The published `site/src/data/us-cnn-summary.v1.json` contains the median of nine separately accounted window returns, maximum drawdowns and cumulative one-way turnover for each method, cost and nominal target. Windows are not claimed to be statistically independent. It excludes the SPY benchmark and all raw inputs and curves.
